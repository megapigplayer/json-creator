## 1. What do real sellers earn per asset/listing, how many assets are needed for $50/month, and how long does it take?

### Takeaway
There is very little verified per-asset earnings data for 2025–2026. The best datapoint is one AI-image contributor's public diary on Adobe Stock: about $0.85 per download and about $10–15/month after 2–3 months. At that rate, $50/month net takes about 65–70 downloads a month, which implies a portfolio in the low thousands of accepted files. Adobe's weekly submission caps (introduced in 2025) make that portfolio slow to build. Etsy digital downloads net about $5 per $6 sale, so only about 12–13 sales a month are needed, but no reliable data show how often a new AI-content shop gets there. Every other channel needs far more volume (Freepik pays $0.04–$0.07 per download; Spotify pays $0.003–$0.005 per stream behind a 1,000-stream gate) or has just cut royalties (Merch on Demand, June 2026).

### Cited Findings
**Adobe Stock (AI images and video)**
- One contributor began uploading AI images to Adobe Stock on Jan 1, 2025. They reported **$10 from 12 downloads after 45 days**, **$22 from 25 downloads after 70 days**, and **~2,000 images in the portfolio by Dec 2025**. Most downloads came from trending or seasonal topics such as Valentine's Day. This is a single self-reported case, and the year-end earnings figure could not be retrieved — [Pranit Naik, Medium (45 days)](https://medium.com/@pranithnaikpranit/earnings-after-45-days-of-uploading-ai-images-to-adobe-stock-started-jan-1-2025-79ca2f6a00f0); [Pranit Naik, Medium (Day 365)](https://medium.com/everyday-ai/day-365-of-uploading-ai-images-on-adobe-stock-af2feee7cad1)
- Adobe pays **33% of the sale price for non-video assets** (photos, illustrations, vectors) and **35% for video**. Royalties are calculated in credits, with 1 credit = 1 USD — [Adobe Stock royalty rates](https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/payments-earnings/royalties-pricing/royalty-rates-assets.html); [Xpiks blog](https://xpiksapp.com/blog/how-much-adobe-stock-pays-contributors/)
- A contributor on Adobe's forum reported earning **~$21 from a single 5-second AI video** (an airport scene). It was their most-downloaded video and, for three months, the most-downloaded AI video from Brazil — [Adobe Community thread](https://community.adobe.com/questions-38/adobe-stock-1556739)
- A YouTube creator claims **"$4,140.40" in Adobe Stock earnings in 2026 so far**. Portfolio size and content mix are unknown and the claim is unverified — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KEceHVyQkiM)
- A Medium post titled "I sold AI images on Adobe Stock for 6 months, this is how much I earned" exists, but its figures could not be retrieved — [Inessa, Medium](https://medium.com/@inesishere/i-tried-selling-ai-images-on-adobe-stock-this-is-how-much-i-earned-51f7da2ed1bb)

**Freepik**
- Freepik pays contributors monthly from a pool: subscription revenue is divided across downloads. One contributor's actual payout averaged **$0.051 per download**, inside a commonly cited **$0.04–$0.07** range. **The same 612 files earned roughly 6x more on Adobe Stock** over the same period, despite fewer downloads **(secondary)**. The search summary did not say which of these two pages holds the 612-file comparison — [AutoKeyWorder Freepik guide](https://autokeyworder.com/blog/freepik-contributor-guide/); [Rastock Freepik guide](https://rastock.ai/blog/freepik-contributor-requirements-and-earnings)
- A contributor published a post on earnings after uploading **13,000 AI images to Freepik**; the amount could not be retrieved — [Pranit Naik, Medium](https://medium.com/mr-plan-publication/earnings-after-uploading-13-000-ai-images-on-freepik-a499c7b2cc35)

**Etsy digital downloads**
- Typical pricing: a single printable sells for **$4–8**, and a bundle of 10 related printables for **$15–25** **(secondary; exact page within these results uncertain)** — [SellerApp Etsy trends](https://www.sellerapp.com/blog/etsy-trends/); [CraftedCharts](https://craftedcharts.com/blogs/marketing/etsy-sales-outlook-2026)
- Anecdote: sellers who averaged **15–30 orders/day in 2024 now average 3–5**, about an 80% decline (anecdotal) — [Seller Nook, Medium](https://medium.com/activated-thinker/is-it-still-worth-selling-digital-products-on-etsy-0f013a762450)
- Etsy had **93.5M active buyers at end-2025** and **$11.9B GMS in 2025** **(secondary, citing Etsy financial reports)** — [CraftedCharts](https://craftedcharts.com/blogs/marketing/whay-etsy-sales-are-down)
- Course-seller headlines such as "Digital Products To Sell on Etsy in 2026 … ($16,980/Mo)" are marketing and subject to survivorship bias; they are not evidence of typical outcomes — [Dylan Jahraus blog](https://dylanjahraus.com/digital-products-to-sell-on-etsy-in-2026-6-top-trends-16980-mo/)

**Print-on-demand**
- TeePublic: most sellers earn **$2–$4 per sale**. Artisan-tier accounts get **$4 per T-shirt at full price and $2 during sitewide sales**; Apprentice-tier accounts get **$3 / $1**. "Top creators" report **$500–$2,000/month after 500+ designs** **(secondary; survivorship bias)** — [Merch Titans TeePublic review 2026](https://merchtitans.com/blog/teepublic-review-2026); [AutoKeyWorder TeePublic vs Redbubble](https://autokeyworder.com/blog/teepublic-vs-redbubble/)
- Merch on Demand after June 1, 2026: a $19.99 T-shirt earns **$2.44 at the default "Creator" tier**, versus $4.88 under the previous flat rate. The "Premium" tier (more than 35% of sales from external traffic) earns **2.16x base, about $5.27** **(secondary; official Amazon text not retrieved)** — [Merch Titans](https://merchtitans.com/blog/amazon-merch-royalty-changes-june-2026); [MyDesigns](https://mydesigns.io/blog/amazon-merch-royalty-changes-2026/)

**AI music / streaming**
- Spotify pays roughly **$0.003–$0.005 per stream** **(secondary estimate)** — [Chartlex](https://www.chartlex.com/blog/money/how-much-does-spotify-pay-per-stream-2026)
- Since early 2024, a track earns recorded royalties only after **at least 1,000 streams in the previous 12 months**, plus a minimum number of unique listeners — [Spotify for Artists: Modernizing Our Royalty System](https://artists.spotify.com/blog/modernizing-our-royalty-system); [Spotify support: Track monetization eligibility](https://support.spotify.com/us/artists/article/track-monetization-eligibility/)
- About **87% of tracks fell below that threshold in 2024**, and more than $47M was redistributed away from them that year **(secondary; unverified)** — [Jack Righteous](https://jackrighteous.com/en-us/blogs/mont-real/spotifys-new-royalty-model-sparks-controversy-indie-artists-in-crisis)
- On Deezer, AI tracks account for only **1–3% of streams**, even though they are about half of uploads — [Deezer Newsroom, Jul 2026](https://newsroom-deezer.com/2026/07/ai-music-exceeds-50-percent-daily-uploads-deezer/); [TechCrunch, Jul 21 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/21/music-streamer-deezer-says-more-than-50-of-daily-uploads-are-ai-generated/)

**Prompt marketplaces**
- PromptBase takes **20% of sales it refers** (seller keeps 80%) and **0% on sales through the seller's own link**. The median prompt price is **$5.99** **(secondary)** — [Cartmango](https://cartmango.com/sell-ai-prompts/); [AI Profit Mode](https://aiprofitmode.com/how-to-sell-on-promptbase.html)
- PromptBase earnings claims contradict each other across pages: "$100–$2,000/month on average", "beginners $50–$200/month after three months", and "the platform's top-40 sellers reportedly clear $200–$400 a month after fees". All are unverified — [NeuraPulse (marketing)](https://neuraplus-ai.github.io/blog/selling-ai-prompts-online-guide.html); [SoftHubTools](https://softhubtools.com/promptbase-review-2026-buy-sell-make-money-ai-prompts/); [Digiko / Market Anarchy](https://digiko.io/ai-money/selling-ai-prompts-is-a-25b-market-read-what-sellers-keep)

### Inferences
- **Adobe Stock.** The diary implies about **$0.83–$0.88 per download** ($10/12 and $22/25).
  - Target: $50 net plus about $5–10/month of generation cost means roughly **$55–60 gross, or about 65–70 downloads a month**.
  - Assumption (unsourced): a mature AI portfolio gets **0.02–0.05 downloads per file per month**. On that basis you need **about 1,300–3,500 accepted files**.
  - With reported weekly caps of about 22–100 submissions for ordinary accounts (Section 2), building that many accepted files takes **roughly 4–18+ months** even at 100% acceptance. Files also need time to start selling after acceptance.
  - Conclusion: **$50/month within 3 months is very unlikely; within 6 months it is unlikely but possible.**
- **Adobe Stock AI video.** Per-download value is plausibly several dollars (one 5-second clip earned about $21 over about 3 months). If so, fewer assets are needed, but generation costs more. This rests on one anecdote.
- **Etsy digital.** A $6 sale in the US nets about **$4.98**: $6 − $0.39 (6.5%) − $0.43 (3% + $0.25) − $0.20 (automatic renewal fee charged on each sale).
  - Catalog upkeep: 200 listings × $0.20 every 4 months is about $10/month if everything auto-renews. The fee applies at expiry, so this is an upper bound.
  - That puts the target at about **12–13 sales per month** to clear $50 net.
- **Volume needed on the other channels:**

| Channel | Assumed rate | Needed for $50/month |
|---|---|---|
| TeePublic | $2–$4 per sale | 13–25 sales |
| Merch on Demand (Creator tier) | $2.44 per $19.99 tee | ~21 sales |
| Freepik | $0.04–$0.07 per download | 700–1,250 downloads |
| Spotify | $0.003–$0.005 per stream | 10,000–17,000 monetized streams, on tracks that each clear 1,000 streams/year |
| PromptBase | $4.79 per sale ($5.99 × 80%) | ~11 sales |

- **Survivorship bias is pervasive.** Every earnings report found is self-selected: people who post income reports skew toward those who earned something, and many also sell courses or tools.

### Gaps
- No platform publishes per-asset earnings distributions. No dataset of revenue per image per month for AI portfolios in 2025–2026 was found; the microstockgroup and Reddit threads could not be read.
- No dataset was found on what share of new Etsy digital shops reach a given number of sales (eRank/Everbee data not retrieved).
- Final 12-month earnings in the Pranit Naik Adobe diary, and his Freepik totals, were not retrieved.
- No per-title earnings data was found for KDP low-content or AI books, Redbubble designs, Creative Market, Canva, Notion templates, itch.io or Unity assets, fonts, 3D models, LUTs, or wallpapers.

## 2. Which 2024–2026 policy changes affect AI content on each platform?

### Takeaway
Nearly every channel tightened between 2024 and 2026, and the changes target exactly the high-volume "generate and upload at scale" approach that AI makes possible:
- **Adobe Stock** added opaque per-account weekly submission caps (May 2025) and is rejecting more AI work as "similar content".
- **KDP** cut new-title creation to **2 per format per week** (effective Sept 21, 2026).
- **Spotify** launched a spam filter and AI disclosure credits (Sept 2025), on top of the 1,000-stream royalty gate.
- **Redbubble** introduced a 50% platform fee for "Standard" accounts (Sept 2025).
- **Merch on Demand** roughly halved royalties for sellers who rely on organic Amazon traffic (June 2026).
- **Etsy** requires AI disclosure and, since June 2025, original designs (no templated designs).
- **Shutterstock** still refuses AI content from contributors.

Disclosure is mandatory on almost every platform that accepts AI content. Undisclosed AI content is a ToS violation, and any method that relies on it is eliminated.

### Cited Findings
**Adobe Stock**
- Adobe accepts AI images, vectors and videos if the contributor ticks **"Created using generative AI tools"**. Contributors should submit only AI content that adds unique value and avoid **multiple similar iterations from the same prompt** — [Adobe gen-AI FAQ](https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/help/generative-ai-faq.html); [Adobe gen-AI content guidelines](https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/submit-your-content/submit-generative-ai-content/generative-ai-content-guidelines.html); [Submit AI-generated content](https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/submit-your-content/submit-generative-ai-content/submit-generative-ai-content.html)
- In **mid-May 2025**, Adobe emailed contributors about new **per-account submission limits** based on past acceptance rates, sales and "other factors". The limits are not published and vary by account — [Adobe Community announcement](https://community.adobe.com/announcements-37/new-submission-limits-for-adobe-stock-contributors-315961); [PetaPixel, May 22 2025](https://petapixel.com/2025/05/22/almost-half-of-the-images-on-adobe-stock-are-ai-generated/); [Meita blog](https://www.meita.ai/blog/adobe-stock-new-limit); [Microstockgroup thread](https://microstockgroup.com/fotolia-com/new-submission-limits-for-adobe-stock-contributors/)
- Later reductions reported by contributors:
  - limits falling from **300 → 100 → as low as 22 per week**;
  - some accounts previously uploaded up to **10,000 per week**, and the highest limit now reported is **1,000 per week**;
  - other contributors report only **30–40 submissions per week**.
  - Sources: [Adobe Community: "Decrease in weekly limits"](https://community.adobe.com/t5/stock-contributors-discussions/decrease-in-weekly-limits-and-asset-uploads/td-p/15545431); [Adobe Community: "only 30-40 submission per week"](https://community.adobe.com/questions-38/why-i-am-having-only-30-40-submission-per-week-limit-i-am-unable-to-submit-more-why-317379); [Adobe Community: "Submission limit reached"](https://community.adobe.com/questions-38/submission-limit-reached-317261); [Meita](https://www.meita.ai/blog/adobe-stock-new-limit)
- Contributors report a sharp rise in rejections citing **"similar content already in our collection"** — [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2025/05/22/almost-half-of-the-images-on-adobe-stock-are-ai-generated/); [CineD](https://www.cined.com/ai-flood-on-adobe-stock-nearly-half-of-all-images-now-ai-generated/)
- No 2026 change to the royalty rates (33% / 35%) or the **$25 payout threshold** was found — [Adobe payment requirements](https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/help/getting-paid.html); [Adobe royalty details](https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/help/royalty-details.html)
- Adobe has paid annual "Firefly bonus" payments since Sept 2023 **(secondary)** — [Xpiks](https://xpiksapp.com/blog/how-much-adobe-stock-pays-contributors/)

**Shutterstock**
- **Shutterstock does not accept AI-generated content from contributors.** Its reasoning is that ownership of AI output cannot be assigned to an individual. It compensates contributors whose work was used to train AI through a **Contributor Fund**, and licenses AI imagery only through its own generator — [Shutterstock contributor FAQ](https://submit.shutterstock.com/help/en/articles/10594676-ai-generated-content-on-shutterstock-contributor-faq); [Shutterstock policy update](https://submit.shutterstock.com/help/en/articles/10594622-content-policy-updates-ai-generated-content)
- One source claims 2026 automated scanning of AI metadata (C2PA, IPTC DigitalSourceType, EXIF) and a **3-strikes-in-12-months permanent ban** **(secondary, unverified)** — [AI Metadata Cleaner blog](https://aimetadatacleaner.com/blog/shutterstock-ai-content-policies-2026-guide)
- **Getty–Shutterstock merger:** agreed Jan 6, 2025; the US DOJ waiting period expired Feb 23, 2026; the UK CMA made clearance conditional on selling Shutterstock's editorial business; Getty declined, and the **merger agreement was terminated (July 7, 2026 per the filings summary)** — [Shutterstock 10-Q, Q2 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001549346/000154934626000029/sstk-20260630.htm); [Shutterstock 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001549346/000114036126028035/ef20077612_8k.htm); [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/getty-images-gety-shutterstock-merger-105429531.html)

**Freepik / Dreamstime / 123RF / Wirestock**
- **Freepik** accepts AI photos, vectors and PSDs from any generator. Every AI asset must carry the **AI tag**, set by checkbox or via **CSV import** — [Freepik support: AI-generated resources guidelines](https://support.freepik.com/s/article/AI-generated-resources-General-guidelines); [AutoKeyWorder](https://autokeyworder.com/blog/freepik-contributor-guide/)
- **Dreamstime** has accepted AI content since Dec 2022. The contributor must hold all rights, the description must say the image is AI-generated, and it must go in the "Illustrations and Clip Art/AI generated" category. **(2022–23 policy – possibly outdated)** — [Dreamstime blog](https://www.dreamstime.com/blog/dreamstime-now-accepting-ai-generated-content-under-specific-terms-59030); [Dreamstime forum](https://www.dreamstime.com/boards/forum-10/thread-53554)
- **123RF** accepts AI content labelled "AI Generated" in the description and keywords, in the "AI Generated Images" category **(older info – possibly outdated)** — [Xpiks](https://xpiksapp.com/blog/ai-images-on-microstocks/); [Jamo Images](https://jamoimages.com/where-you-can-sell-ai-generated-stock-images/)
- **Wirestock**: submitting only to Wirestock's own marketplace is free; distributing to other agencies requires paying **(older, low-quality source)** — [Toolify](https://www.toolify.ai/ai-news/turn-your-ai-art-into-profit-with-wirestock-2109010)

**Etsy**
- AI-generated digital products are allowed. Under the 2024 **Creativity Standards**, designs the seller created by prompting AI belong under **"Designed by a seller"**, and AI use must be **disclosed in the listing description** **(secondary summaries of Etsy policy; the official page was not read)** — [Promptless Press](https://www.promptlesspress.com/blog-etsy-ai-policy-2026-digital-products); [iscompliant](https://iscompliant.app/Blog/etsy-ai-digital-downloads); [Ngini](https://ngini.com/en-us/blog/etsy-ai-disclosure-policy-2026-explained); [LzyPOD](https://lzypod.com/blog/etsy-ai-policy-what-sellers-need-to-know/)
- **Since June 2025**, items must be "based on a seller's original design". The exception for **templated designs, purchased design bundles and pre-made designs (even with commercial licences)** was removed. Pattern and digital-download sellers using AI visuals (SVGs, planners, printables) are among those facing enforcement, which is reportedly stricter in 2025–26, with automated detection plus member reports **(secondary)** — [iscompliant (digital downloads)](https://iscompliant.app/Blog/etsy-ai-digital-downloads); [iscompliant (POD guide)](https://iscompliant.app/Blog/etsy-creativity-standards-pod-sellers-guide); [BulkMockup](https://www.bulkmockup.com/etsy-policy-changes/)
- **New-shop onboarding:** Etsy charges some new sellers a one-time, non-refundable **setup fee of $15**, **up to about $29** depending on region or verification needs, as part of stronger fraud checks — [Etsy Seller Handbook: Strengthening New-Shop Onboarding](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1241780194948); [eRank Help](https://help.erank.com/blog/understanding-etsys-new-15-setup-fee/); [LitCommerce](https://litcommerce.com/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-start-an-etsy-shop/)

**Creative Market**
- Creative Market shows an **AI label**. It defines an AI-generated product as one whose key feature or file was primarily created with gen-AI tools (Midjourney, DALL·E, Firefly, …), such as AI stock photos or AI-based mockups, and it asks shop owners to disclose — [Creative Market Support: New AI Label](https://support.creativemarket.com/hc/en-us/articles/26926388691099-Navigating-Our-New-AI-Label)
- In 2025 it announced a **"Product Differentiation Policy"** to promote "truly innovative design" — [Creative Market Blog: Destination Design 2025](https://creativemarket.com/blog/destination-design-vision)

**Redbubble**
- **From Sept 1, 2025**, accounts fall into three tiers:
  - **Standard: 50% platform fee** on monthly earnings;
  - **Premium: 20%**;
  - **Pro: 0%**.
  - Fees are capped at **$150/€150/£150 per payment period**.
  - Tier depends on design quality, artistic skill, volume of designs, account activity, commercial success and guideline compliance.
  - An "excess markup" fee applies on Standard and Premium when markup exceeds 20%.
  - Sources: [Redbubble Blog, Aug 2025](https://blog.redbubble.com/2025/08/artist-account-tiers-and-fees/); [Redbubble Help: how tier determines fee](https://help.redbubble.com/hc/en-us/articles/50959863016724-How-does-my-Account-Tier-determine-my-platform-fee); [What account fees exist](https://help.redbubble.com/hc/en-us/articles/50960130992916-What-account-fees-exist-on-Redbubble); [What are Account Tiers](https://help.redbubble.com/hc/en-us/articles/50959744156948-What-are-Account-Tiers)
- The payout **threshold dropped from $20 to $10 (effective July 1, 2026)**. A **$3/month "Unpayable Artist Account Fee"** applies when payment details are invalid and the balance is $10 or more. The search summary did not say which of these pages holds each item — [Redbubble Help: fee](https://help.redbubble.com/hc/en-us/articles/49160894885012-How-much-is-the-fee-and-when-does-it-apply); [Redbubble Blog: Changes to Yearly Artist Payments](https://blog.redbubble.com/2024/12/changes-to-yearly-artist-payments/)
- There is no blanket AI ban; AI output is judged by the same content rules. Mass-uploading low-quality AI art can land an account in the Standard tier or get it suspended **(secondary)** — [MyPromptHaven](https://myprompthaven.com/redbubble-ai-generated-art-policy-2026-official-rules-and-guidelines/)

**TeePublic / Spreadshirt**
- Neither publishes an explicit AI policy; general IP and content rules apply, and Spreadshirt's position is unclear **(secondary)** — [MerchBanao](https://merchbanao.com/blog/ai-art-for-print-on-demand/); [AI Tools Arena](https://aitoolsarena.com/text-to-image/9-websites-to-sell-your-ai-art)

**Merch on Demand (Amazon)**
- There is no separate published AI rule. AI-assisted designs go through the same IP, copycat, content, metadata and quality checks as any other **(secondary)** — [MerchBanao: Amazon Merch AI policy](https://merchbanao.com/blog/amazon-merch-ai-policy/); [AMZPrep](https://amzprep.com/amazon-merch-on-demand/)
- It is invite/application-based. New accounts start at **Tier 10** (10 designs) and move up through 25 → 100 → 500 → 1,000 → 2,000 → 4,000 → 8,000+. Application approval rates are about **30–40% in 2026**, reportedly lower after a wave of AI spam accounts **(secondary, unverified)** — [AMZPrep](https://amzprep.com/amazon-merch-on-demand/); [The Selling Guys](https://www.thesellingguys.com/merch-by-amazon/)
- **"Royalty incentive groups" from June 1, 2026.** Royalty now depends on the trailing two-month share of sales driven by **non-organic (external) traffic**: **Creator** (default), **Plus** (15% or more external), **Premium** (more than 35% external). Sellers who rely on organic Amazon traffic see royalties cut about 50% **(secondary; consistent across several Merch-tool sites, but Amazon's own text was not retrieved)** — [Merch Titans](https://merchtitans.com/blog/amazon-merch-royalty-changes-june-2026); [MyDesigns](https://mydesigns.io/blog/amazon-merch-royalty-changes-2026/); [Seller Sprite](https://sellersprite.ai/en/blog/amazon-merch-royalties-and-pricing); [Pixels N' Profits](https://pixelsnprofits.com/amazon-merch-worth-it-2026/)

**Amazon KDP**
- Authors must **disclose AI-generated text, images or translations** with a checkbox during publishing. "AI-assisted" work (content you created and refined with AI) needs no disclosure. The disclosure is private; there is no public AI badge as of 2026 — [Authors Guild](https://authorsguild.org/news/amazon-adds-to-kdp-generative-ai-policy-caps-daily-self-publishing-uploads/); [Publishing.co.uk](https://publishing.co.uk/guides/kdp-ai-content-disclosure/); [Univers Studio](https://www.univers.studio/blog/kdp-ai-content-policy-2026/)
- **Title-creation caps**, in sequence:
  1. **3 new titles per day** — the Authors Guild reported Amazon "caps daily self-publishing uploads".
  2. **Late 2025**: replaced by **10 per format per week**.
  3. **From Monday, Sept 21, 2026**: **2 new titles per format per week** (eBook, paperback, hardcover — at most 6 per week). The limit resets Sundays at 00:00 UTC, existing titles are unaffected, and exception language was removed from KDP help pages on Sept 22, 2026.
  - Sources: [KDP Community: Update on KDP Title Creation Limits](https://www.kdpcommunity.com/s/article/KDP-Title-Creation-Limits-Update?language=en_US&forum=KDP+Forum); [Publishers Lunch, Sept 2026](https://lunch.publishersmarketplace.com/2026/09/kdp-limits-authors-to-two-new-titles-a-week/); [PublishFlow](https://publishflow.ai/knowledge-hub/blog/kdp-title-creation-limit/); [AuthorClimb](https://www.authorclimb.com/blog/amazon-kdp-upload-limit); [SFF Chronicles: "Amazon KDP limits publishers to fight AI spam"](https://www.sffchronicles.com/threads/591577/)
- **Conflict:** one 2026 guide in the search results says the 3-per-day cap was "introduced in late 2024". The search summary did not identify which page; candidates include [Built&Written](https://www.builtwritten.com/blog/disclose-ai-in-your-book-kdp-policy-2026) and [KDP Builder](https://kdpbuilder.com/blog/kdp-ai-disclosure-rules). ## 3. How saturated are these markets with AI content, and is buyer demand declining?

### Takeaway
Supply is flooded, and the marketplaces themselves now sell AI generation to buyers:
- **Adobe Stock** was about **48% AI images by April 2025**.
- **Deezer** passed **50% AI of daily uploads in July 2026**, yet AI tracks draw only 1–3% of streams.
- **Spotify** removed 75M spam tracks in a year.
- **KDP** has cut new-title allowances twice to fight AI spam.
- **Adobe, Shutterstock and Freepik** all offer on-platform generative tools to customers, which competes directly with contributors selling generic AI stock.

Demand for *generic* AI imagery, music and prompts is being commoditized away. What still sells is specific, well-keyworded, seasonal or niche material.

## 4. Which steps can be fully automated versus requiring human work, and is automated uploading permitted?

### Takeaway
Only **Etsy** offers a documented, officially sanctioned API for creating listings and attaching digital files. That makes Etsy the one marketplace where an AI agent can legitimately run everything from generation to publishing. **Freepik** supports CSV bulk metadata import. **Adobe Stock** likely supports scripted file delivery (FTP/SFTP plus CSV, from prior knowledge – verify), but the final submission, with its AI and "fictional people/property" declarations, is a portal step, and weekly caps limit volume regardless. KDP, Merch on Demand, Redbubble, TeePublic and DistroKid have no public upload API. Driving their web interfaces with bots is at best a ToS grey area, and platforms explicitly penalize mass or bulk uploading, so treat those channels as needing manual uploads.

### Cited Findings

**Reasoning behind the two MAYBEs.**
- **Adobe Stock**
  - *For:* Adobe supplies the buyers, pays about $0.85 per image download (about 6x Freepik for the same files), requires no customer service, and pays out at $25. A single agent pipeline can cover generation, quality checks, keywording and file delivery.
  - *Against:* weekly caps (22–100 per week reported), rising "similar content" rejections, and an unknown and falling revenue per image. Assets also take months to start selling, so $50/month by month 3 is near-impossible and by month 6 unlikely (about 10–15%).
  - Income, once built, tends to persist month to month, with seasonal swings.
- **Etsy digital downloads**
  - *For:* only about 12–13 sales a month are needed, the listing and publishing pipeline is fully API-driven and officially sanctioned, and Etsy has a very large buyer base (93.5M).
  - *Against:* saturated categories, new-shop discoverability, mandatory AI disclosure (which some buyers avoid), stricter originality enforcement since June 2025, and buyer messages that still need a human. The downside variance is high: many new digital shops make zero sales. Probability about 15–20% by month 6, with no reliable base rate.

## 7. Which 1–2 options are most likely to reach $50/month net in 3–6 months with near-zero ongoing effort? (Survivors: setup, fees, evidence)

### Takeaway
The two survivors are **(1) Adobe Stock, with Freepik as an add-on for the same files**, and **(2) Etsy AI-designed digital downloads published through the official Open API v3.** Both are **MAYBE**, and both are unlikely to reach $50/month net by month 3. By month 6 my combined odds are roughly **25–30%** if both run in parallel from one shared AI asset pipeline. The assets can be reused across both because Adobe Stock licensing is non-exclusive (prior knowledge – verify). Neither is truly zero-touch: Adobe needs a weekly capped submission click (unless automation is confirmed as permitted), and Etsy needs occasional replies to buyer messages.

### Cited Findings
**Survivor 1 – Adobe Stock (AI illustrations, backgrounds, vectors, optional short AI video)**

### Inferences
**Concrete setup – Survivor 1 (Adobe Stock + Freepik)**
1. **One-time human steps:**
   - create an Adobe ID and join Adobe Stock Contributor;
   - complete the tax form (W-8BEN if non-US – verify);
   - link a payout account (PayPal/Skrill – verify);
   - optionally generate SFTP credentials (verify);
   - create a Freepik contributor account the same way.
2. **Agent pipeline, weekly** (sized to the account's cap):
   1. Pick seasonal and commercial themes 6–10 weeks ahead: holidays, business concepts, textures and backgrounds, isolated objects. Avoid real people, brands, logos, text errors and artist-style prompts.
   2. Generate with a tool whose terms grant commercial rights, then upscale to Adobe's size minimum (4MP – verify).
   3. Run automated vision QA to reject artifacts, and a near-duplicate filter so the batch does not trigger "similar content" rejections.
   4. Write titles and up to 49 keywords (limit – verify) into a CSV, and upload via SFTP (verify).
   5. **Human:** a weekly 10–20 minute submit in the portal with the AI box ticked, unless Adobe's terms are confirmed to allow automation.
   6. Push the same files to Freepik with the AI tag via CSV.
3. **Costs:** about $0–30/month for generation. Keep it at or below about $10/month so it doesn't consume the $50 target, e.g. local or open models or cheap API image calls.
4. **Kill / continue test:** at month 4, if Adobe is under $10/month and fewer than about 20 downloads/month, stop. Also stop if the cap stays below about 30/week despite good acceptance rates.
5. **Expected trajectory:** first sales within 2–8 weeks; about $10–20/month by month 3 and about $20–50/month by month 6 in a decent case (inference from one diary). Hitting $50 net by month 6 is about 10–15%.

**Concrete setup – Survivor 2 (Etsy digital downloads via API)**
1. **One-time human steps:**
   - open the shop (possible $15–29 fee), complete ID verification, connect a bank account, enable 2FA;
   - register an Etsy developer app and obtain an API key (approval process – verify);
   - authorize OAuth with `listings_r` / `listings_w`;
   - approve the shop's policies text.
2. **Agent pipeline:**
   1. Pick niches: evergreen, specific printables (e.g., classroom and homeschool worksheets, niche planners, trackers, party printables, nursery wall-art sets). Use Etsy search autocomplete and competitor review counts; a paid research tool is optional.
   2. Generate artwork with AI, but lay out text-heavy products (planners, worksheets) programmatically so the text is error-free.
   3. Create 5–10 mockups per listing.
   4. Package the files as PDF/PNG/ZIP within Etsy's file limits (verify).
   5. Publish through the API: `createDraftListing` (digital type), upload images and files, then activate. Every description includes a clear AI-use disclosure, and the "Designed by" category is set correctly.
   6. Start with about 50–150 listings (about $10–30 in listing fees) and add a few per week rather than hundreds at once, to limit spam and velocity risk (unverified).
3. **Human, ongoing:** reply to buyer messages (Etsy's app sends notifications; the API likely does not cover messaging – verify), and deal with any policy flags.
4. **Kill / continue test:** at month 3, if there are fewer than 3 sales/month across 100+ listings, stop and avoid paying renewal fees.
5. **Expected trajectory:** highly variable. Needs about 12–13 sales/month at $6 for $50 net. My estimate is about 8% by month 3 and 15–20% by month 6.

**Running both together.** Generation and QA are shared. For example, Adobe background and texture sets can be repackaged as Etsy "digital paper" packs, provided Adobe's non-exclusive terms allow selling the same images elsewhere (verify). Because the two outcomes are only partly correlated, the chance that at least one reaches $50/month by month 6 is about **25–30%**.
